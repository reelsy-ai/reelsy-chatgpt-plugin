# Reelsy Ecommerce Product Ad

## Domain Method

1. Identify the product, audience, platform, target duration, offer, and desired action. Ask only for facts that materially change the result.
2. Inspect provided media before describing packaging, labels, colors, materials, scale, use context, or visible results. Never invent product claims.
3. Choose a compact conversion structure: immediate product recognition, one concrete problem or desire, one demonstrable mechanism or proof point, a believable result, and a clear CTA.
4. Define continuity anchors for the product package, label, color, material, scale, hero person, wardrobe, location, and lighting.
5. Put exact prices, labels, disclaimers, logos, proof text, captions, and CTA copy in Editing layers instead of paid generated footage.
6. Produce a compact production intent with objective, aspect ratio, duration, ordered beats, continuity anchors, must-preserve facts, prohibited claims, native-audio policy, and completion criteria.

## Capability Routing

- Use `$reelsy-video-production` when new video or image assets are required.
- Use `$reelsy-video-editing` for product titles, prices, badges, captions, stickers, overlays, music, transitions, trims, ordering, and revised Finals.
- Use both when producing a new campaign-ready video.

## Quality Rules

- The product must be recognizable in the opening moment.
- Keep packaging, labels, colors, materials, and scale stable unless the user explicitly requests a redesign.
- Show only observable or user-supplied benefits. Do not imply unsupported medical, financial, safety, or performance claims.
- Prefer the fewest generated clips that preserve a coherent demonstration. Split when product identity, scene, time, or camera logic cannot remain continuous.
- Do not burn exact text into generated frames when a deterministic Editing layer can render it.
